How to use the hub safely

Search by commission, exam, post name or stage. Always open the official source before applying or preparing a case-specific syllabus because eligibility, dates and paper patterns can change between advertisements.

Application-to-exam checklist

Use this sequence for a recruitment advertisement or competitive examination. The commission's current advertisement and rules always take precedence over summaries or older preparation material.

1. Match the exact post or exam

Record the advertisement number, post title, domicile/provincial quota where applicable, required degree and any experience or age conditions. Similar post names can have different eligibility rules.

2. Read the current syllabus and rules

Open the advertisement and linked syllabus for that specific case or examination. Note compulsory subjects, optional choices, marks, screening stages, document requirements and any later corrigendum.

3. Submit through the official portal

Use the portal named in the notice, enter details consistently with your documents and follow the stated fee method. Avoid agents promising selection or asking for account credentials.

4. Save evidence and check status

Keep the submitted form, application or case number, payment proof and confirmation. Revisit the official candidate portal for roll-number slips, test venue, schedule changes and missing-document notices.

5. Prepare from the relevant stage

Build a plan around the official syllabus and available time. Use past papers for practice, but treat them as examples rather than a promise that the next paper will repeat the same questions.

6. Verify results and next deadlines

Check written results, interview calls, merit lists and final recommendations on the responsible commission's site. Keep checking official notices after each stage; qualifying one stage may not complete the process.

Before you pay: confirm the advertisement is current, the application URL belongs to the responsible authority, the fee instructions match the notice, and the closing date has not changed.
