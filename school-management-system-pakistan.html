School Operations in Pakistan

A practical school management system for Pakistan

EduNizam is designed for schools and education institutes that want day-to-day administration, academics and communication in one role-based workspace instead of scattered registers, spreadsheets and chat threads.

Login / Sign Up

Student and academic administration

Maintain student profiles, classes, sections, attendance, results, report cards, behavior records, documents and progress views. Head and teacher access is separated so users see tools and records relevant to their role.

Fees, finance and payroll

Create class-wise fee structures, monthly challans and receipts, track paid and pending amounts, maintain a school cashbook, and manage staff attendance and payroll records. Printable documents can carry the institute's own name and logo.

Timetables, exams and daily work

Build class timetables and exam date sheets, manage lesson plans and syllabus progress, publish notices, handle homework and leave requests, and keep school events and functions visible to the appropriate audience.

Communication and school workflows

EduNizam includes linked messaging, parent complaint notices, helpdesk tickets, school notices and role-based communication. Cloud mode uses authenticated relationships so parent, student, teacher and head views can remain separated.

Local-first and cloud-ready

The app can demonstrate workflows locally in the browser and can use Supabase for authenticated cross-device records. Institutions can adopt modules gradually instead of replacing every existing process at once.
