Admissions Workflow

Online admissions from application to enrollment

EduNizam's admissions workflow is built to reduce repeated manual entry while keeping the institute in control of program setup, application review, fee requirements and final enrollment.

Start Admission Application

Complete student application

Applicants select an existing school, create an applicant account, enter student and guardian details, previous education, address and other requested information, then upload the required private documents. A submitted application receives a reference for tracking.

Admin approval before fee

The school reviews the application and documents first. A fee challan is issued only after approval for fee. The applicant can then submit a payment method, transaction/reference and payment proof for Admin verification.

Review and merit workflow

Authorized staff can review applications, record test or interview information when used by the institute, update status and maintain a clear decision trail. Merit tools support weighted review without forcing one admission policy on every institution.

Payment verification and admission confirmation

Admission is confirmed only after payment is verified. The workflow can then generate an admission number, confirmation/enrollment output and connect the student to the school record without pretending an unverified payment is complete.

Connected student record

Finalized admissions can create or connect the student's core school record, reducing duplicate entry between admissions and ongoing school management.
