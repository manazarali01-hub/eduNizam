One digital workspace for education management

Built for gradual adoption

EduNizam supports a local browser mode for demonstration and a cloud-ready mode for authenticated, multi-device use. Cloud features require an institute-managed backend configuration and approved user links.

EduNizam is an independent education-management product. Board, university and curriculum names identify supported academic categories and do not imply official affiliation.

What EduNizam does — and what it does not

EduNizam brings school-management workflows and public learning links into one interface. The public Learning Hub is a navigation and study-support resource; it is not the issuing authority for admissions, examinations, scholarships, qualifications or results.

One starting point

Use the hub to find the relevant official portal, organize a study route and move between connected education topics without treating a directory entry as an official announcement.

Official sources decide

Eligibility, deadlines, fees, recognition, exam patterns and result records are controlled by the relevant institution or regulator. Confirm current details at that source before acting.

No implied affiliation

References to boards, universities, councils and public programmes identify relevant education categories. They do not imply endorsement, partnership or official status.

How to use EduNizam responsibly

Start with the page that matches your task, follow its official-source links, and compare the details against the latest notice or prospectus. Keep your own application receipts and confirmation numbers; do not share passwords, one-time codes or private student records in public channels.

If a link appears outdated or a notice conflicts with an authority's current instructions, rely on the authority and report the issue through the appropriate EduNizam support route. For account and school data handling, read the Privacy page.
