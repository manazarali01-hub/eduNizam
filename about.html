Pakistan + Global Education Platform

One digital workspace for education management

EduNizam is built in Pakistan for schools, colleges and universities locally and worldwide, bringing administration, academic planning and communication into one responsive platform. Its public Learning Hub remains focused on Pakistan-specific learning resources.

Who EduNizam is for

Institute Heads

Manage students, staff, fees, finance, admissions, schedules, notices and operational records.

Teachers

Work with attendance, results, lesson plans, homework, assigned students and communication tools.

Students & Parents

View relevant academic records, notices, schedules, learning resources and approved messages.

Built for gradual adoption

EduNizam supports a local browser mode for demonstration and a cloud-ready mode for authenticated, multi-device use. Cloud features require an institute-managed backend configuration and approved user links.

EduNizam is an independent education-management product. Board, university and curriculum names identify supported academic categories and do not imply official affiliation.
