One platform for institute operations and academics

EduNizam is designed to reduce scattered registers, spreadsheets and disconnected school workflows by bringing the most-used tasks into one consistent digital system.

Attendance with accountabilityAdmins and assigned teachers can mark student attendance, while teacher attendance remains individually controlled with Admin oversight.
Fees, results and recordsKeep student records, fees, results, report cards and school operations connected to the correct institution and role.
Admissions and approvalsApplicants select an existing school, submit an application and follow the process while the School Admin controls final review and decisions.
Role-based accessAdmin, Teacher, Parent and Student experiences are separated so each user sees the school information relevant to their approved role.
