Core capabilities

  • Student administration: profiles, classes, sections, documents and behavior records.
  • Attendance and analytics: daily records, monthly percentages and low-attendance alerts.
  • Staff time and training: exact check-in/check-out, payroll attendance, CPD progress and certificates.
  • Bulk school setup: validated CSV/Excel import for students, staff, classes and sections.
  • Fees and finance: class fee structures, challans, receipts, payroll and cashbook reporting.
  • Academic records: results, report cards, lesson plans and syllabus progress.
  • Timetables and date sheets: class schedules, clash checks and print-ready exam plans.
  • Admissions: complete student applications and private documents, Admin review, post-approval fee challan, payment verification and final admission confirmation.
  • Leave requests: Student and Teacher requests with dates, automatic day count, reason, optional private attachment, Teacher review and Admin final decision.
  • Communication: notices, linked messaging, complaints, meetings and notifications.
  • School community: functions calendar plus institution-only student activities, achievements, pictures, audio and videos.
  • Free student learning: Pakistan board papers, Grade 5 and 8 resources, university material, VU workspace and practice tools are available without a school account through Guest Mode.
  • AI assistance: controlled server-side generation and question-solving when the AI backend is configured.
  • Progressive web app: responsive layout, install support and offline caching for core files.
