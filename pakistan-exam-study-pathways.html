Official-Source Guided Preparation

Pakistan exam and study pathways

Each pathway follows the same useful order: verify the official syllabus or policy, register through the issuing authority, prepare by the correct subjects and pattern, obtain candidate/test information, sit the exam or complete semester activities, then use the official result, merit or admission route.

Medical, engineering and university entry tests

MDCAT

PM&DC syllabus → registration/current notice → Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English and Logical Reasoning preparation → test → result/admitting-university merit process.

PM&DC syllabus

ECAT

Programme requirement → approved subject combination → UET registration → 100-question timed preparation → score → admission/merit process.

NUST NET

Select NET category → verify subject weightings → register for a series → HSSC-based MCQ preparation → result/selection status.

Official NET weightings

HEC admission tests

USAT

Identify the correct stream → open its official content weightage → register through HEC ETC → reasoning, discipline and writing preparation → result for the accepting institution.

HEC content weightages

HAT

Select HAT-I/II/III/IV/General from your 16-year field → use the official Verbal/Analytical/Quantitative weightage → register in ETC → timed 100-MCQ reasoning practice → keys/result → MS/MPhil/PhD or scholarship use.

HEC HAT weightages

LAT

Current ETC notice → official LAT weightage → 75-MCQ section plus essay/personal statement preparation → candidate information → result and university admission.

CSS, FPSC and PPSC

CSS

Current rules/advertisement → MPT → compulsory and optional written syllabus → admission certificate/written exam → psychological assessment → viva → final result.

FPSC General Recruitment

Exact advertisement/case number → case-specific GR syllabus → online application → admission certificate/test → shortlisting/interview → nomination/result.

PPSC

Advertisement/case number → exact case syllabus and planner → application → written/competitive examination → interview where applicable → written/final result and merit.
