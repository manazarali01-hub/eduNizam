Learning Resources

Pakistan-focused learning resources inside the same education workspace

EduNizam combines school operations with searchable learning support so students and teachers can move from records and schedules to study material without using a separate system for every task.

Open Free Learning HubLogin / Sign Up
No school account needed: the public Learning Hub is free for general students. School approval is required only for private school records such as attendance, fees and results.

Pakistan board and assessment resources

The learning area is structured around board and assessment categories, including past-paper discovery and dedicated Grade 5 and Grade 8 resource areas. Source labels help distinguish official, verified and community material.

Virtual University workspace

The VU section is organized for course-based study with past papers, handouts, notes, quizzes, midterm and final-term resources. It is intended as a focused workspace rather than an unstructured file list.

University results, LMS and study portals

The University Hub now covers AIOU, Punjab University, BZU, UOG, UAF, GCUF, University of Sargodha and additional major universities, with official result, LMS/CMS, admission, examination, merit-list and past-paper routes where available. HEC recognition, qualification-register and degree-attestation links are also included.

CSS, PPSC, FPSC and competitive exams

The competitive-exams directory brings together official federal and provincial commission resources plus NTS, ETEA and STS. Students can reach jobs, online applications, syllabi, past papers, roll-number/admission certificates, schedules, results and merit lists without relying on an unofficial vacancy/result claim.

Virtual University course-level search

The VU section goes beyond a generic list: exact course-code searches can surface official course routes plus clearly labelled handouts, highlighted handouts, quizzes, assignments and exam-preparation sources.

Guided exam and study pathways

EduNizam now provides structured official-source pathways for MDCAT, ECAT, NUST NET, HEC USAT/HAT/LAT, CSS, FPSC, PPSC, Virtual University and AIOU. Pathways now continue into subject/topic maps, built-in practice where available, weak-topic history and a Study / Revise / Practice coverage tracker while time-sensitive notices remain tied to official sources.

Practice and AI-assisted learning

Practice tests and weak-topic tracking can complement stored results. When a secure server-side AI backend is configured, the AI Assistant can support question solving and explanations without placing a secret API key in the browser.

Responsible source use

EduNizam is independent from boards and universities. Institution and board names identify academic categories only. Users should verify community material against the latest official syllabus, handouts and examination guidance.
